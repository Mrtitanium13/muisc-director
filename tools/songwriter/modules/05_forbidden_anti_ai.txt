# FORBIDDEN / ANTI-AI PATTERNS

Detect and rewrite:
- Generic metaphors (shattered dreams, burning inside, etc.)
- Empty intensifiers stacked (so very deeply truly)
- Robotic transitions (Meanwhile, Suddenly, In that moment)
- Predictable AABB with no surprise
- Forced emotion / Hallmark wording
- Overly formal grammar in casual genres
- Unnatural dialogue
- Repeated adjectives
- Abstract nouns with no image (destiny, forevermore, echoes)

Policy: remove or rewrite unless user explicitly requested the phrase.
See forbidden_phrases.json for the canonical list.
