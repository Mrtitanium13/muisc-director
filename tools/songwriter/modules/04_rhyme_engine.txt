# RHYME ENGINE RULES

Supported techniques:
- Perfect rhyme
- Slant / near rhyme
- Internal rhyme
- Multisyllabic rhyme
- Assonance / consonance

Rules:
- Never sacrifice meaning or natural speech for a rhyme.
- Match rhyme density to genre (trap denser than folk; pop moderate; EDM hooks often simpler).
- Avoid identical end-words across consecutive lines unless intentional mantra.
- Prefer musical stress alignment over dictionary stress when they conflict for singing.
- If a line only exists to rhyme, delete it and rewrite.
