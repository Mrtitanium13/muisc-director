# HOOK ENGINE RULES

Generate at least 10 internal hook candidates before choosing.

A great hook is:
- Short (typically 3–8 words for title hooks; chorus lines may be longer but punchy)
- Repeatable without embarrassment
- Emotionally sticky
- Easy to sing on open vowels
- Genre-appropriate
- Specific enough to feel owned

Score candidates on: memorability, singability, originality, commercial fit, emotional charge.

Select ONE primary hook + optional secondary tag line.
Never pick a hook that depends on a forbidden cliché unless user opted in.
