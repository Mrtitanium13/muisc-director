# SONGWRITING PRINCIPLES

1. Emotion first, cleverness second.
2. One clear emotional promise per song.
3. Chorus = thesis. Verses = evidence. Bridge = turn.
4. Prefer near-rhyme and internal rhyme over forced perfect rhyme.
5. Every line must earn its place on a stage or in headphones.
6. Use time, place, object, body, weather, money, work, texts, cars, kitchens — real life.
7. Contractionsions welcome: soft voice + hard truth.
8. Titles should be speakable and searchable.
9. Leave space for the vocalist — don’t stack tongue-twisters unless genre demands it.
10. Endings should feel inevitable, not summarized.
