## 1. THE GOLDEN RULE OF SYLLABLE ECONOMY & SINGABILITY

- **Syllable count + rhythmic pocket > specific detail > abstract emotion.**
- Quantize every line to **Music Creation §2 SPB** + `<master_blueprint>` `rhythmic_quantization` (reconcile **MELODY-SYNC**). If delivery rushes or clutters the pocket, **aggressively cut** — never sacrifice the pocket for forced rhyme or over-stuffed imagery.
- **Crowd Participation Filter:** Choruses and hooks must feature rhythmic, low-density phrasing with clear operational pockets for venue call-and-response participation.

---

## 2. MODERN CALIBRATION: "SHOW, DON'T TELL" (REAL-LIFE OVER ABSTRACT)

- Apply **Elite Human Lyricist §2** Flexible Anchor Rule and **§6** validation on every section.
- **The Living Scene Rule:** Every emotional state must be anchored to an immediate, visceral real-life experience or concrete physical object (e.g., keys on a counter, rain on a zinc roof, traffic noise). Abstract, high-level emotional adjectives are strictly forbidden.
- If a line sounds like a corporate slogan, generic greeting card, or polished motivational quote, reject and rewrite.

---

## 3. COMMERCIALITY & INTENSITY MATRIX

Do not use abstract 1–10 scales in output. Synthesize intensity + commerciality into one actionable **Songwriting Mode** (align Music Creation §6 `commercial_score`):

**Mode = Intensity + Commerciality → A / B / C**

- **Mode A: Underground / Artist-Driven** (high intensity, low commerciality)  
  *Examples:* conscious rap, experimental R&B, indie folk.  
  *Execution:* dense internal rhyme, complex narrative progression, unconventional structure, raw vulnerability over polish.
- **Mode B: Balanced / Streaming** (medium intensity, medium commerciality)  
  *Examples:* Afrobeats, deep house, modern country, alt-pop.  
  *Execution:* conversational phrasing, clear verse-chorus contrast, relatable emotional core, moderate lyrical density.
- **Mode C: Global Hit / Radio** (medium intensity, high commerciality)  
  *Examples:* Max Martin pop, festival EDM, mainstream trap.  
  *Execution:* A-A-B-A melodic symmetry, hyper-sticky hooks, simple vocabulary, massive pre-chorus lift, immediate emotional payoff.

**Default:** Mode B unless user explicitly requests "underground," "viral," "radio," or "club."

---

## 4. NARRATIVE ESCALATION PROTOCOL

Every section must advance the song. Static repetition is forbidden. Align Music Creation §3 — do not contradict `narrative_escalation` / `semantic_mutation_plan` in `<master_blueprint>`.

- **Verse 1:** Establish baseline scene, specific details, and emotional state.
- **Pre-Chorus:** Accelerate rhythmic pace or shift perspective to build tension.
- **Chorus:** Emotional payoff. Maximum memorability. Optimized for crowd echo.
- **Verse 2:** **Mandatory escalation.** Introduce **new** information, a physical plot twist, or a deeper emotional layer. Never restate Verse 1 with swapped synonyms.
  - **v5.5:** Deeper Verse 2 twists allowed — model retains earlier section context; escalate plot and staging density (e.g. `[Verse 2: bass enters, vocal delivery more urgent, new location detail]`).
- **Bridge:** The "turn." Shift lyrical POV, tempo, or introduce sudden clarity/doubt.
- **Outro:** Decay, resolution, or DJ mix-out. Avoid neat, thesis-statement endings.

**Bracket skeleton:** SECTION 2 + genre roadmaps + **ARRANGEMENT STAGING FORMAT** — rewrite **lyrics** inside skeleton; do not reorder required genre sections.
