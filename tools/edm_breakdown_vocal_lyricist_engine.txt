# EDM BREAKDOWN VOCAL LYRICIST ENGINE (Techno · Trance · House · Festival EDM)

**When:** Primary genre or fusion matches a vocal EDM lane below **and** Block 2 has vocals. **Overrides** generic EDM poetry (infinite skies, neon overdrive, frequency metaphors) for breakdown/build-up placement.

**Scope:** Block 2 lyrics confined to **[Breakdown]**, **[Build-up]** / **[Build]**, and sparse **[Outro]** tags only — no crowded verse grids. Drops stay instrumental or chop-ready hooks per **MELODY-SYNC § CORE MATRIX LOOKUP TABLE**.

**Sub-profile routing:** Runtime resolves **peak_time_techno** · **vocal_trance** · **deep_melodic** from genre labels (see runtime injectors).

---

## UNIVERSAL ANTI-AI GUARDRAILS (ALL EDM BREAKDOWN PROFILES)

1. **BAN SCI-FI & RAVE METAPHORS:** Never use *frequency, static tension, vibrations, dissolving, galaxies, starlight, seismic, neon* (and close variants: cosmic, interstellar, wavelength, dimension, transcend the night). These read as robotic club clichés.
2. **HUMAN AUTHENTICITY (MANDATORY):** Write exactly how a real person thinks or speaks when vulnerable, hyper-focused, or flooded — short blunt sentences, thought fragments, jagged conversational phrasing (*"Don't say anything," "If you touch me, it's over," "I can hear my heart"*). Focus on **immediate psychological state**. Invent conflict for THIS song; never paste festival slogans.
3. **BAN AI FESTIVAL / HALLMARK FILLS:** *we are thunder, rise up, burn it down, take me higher, break free, set me free, we carry on, hold the line, louder than before, holding on, broken inside, pieces of me, drowning in, lost in the dark, find myself, this is real, in this moment, forever young, chasing dreams, feel the beat, hands up*. If a line could sit on any EDM track, rewrite.
4. **VOCAL PLACEMENT:** Low-register, dry vocals — spoken or whispered tight against the microphone capsule with ultra-close-mic intimateness, high-compression proximity effect, and detailed chest resonance unless the sub-profile calls for floating sung hooks. Keep a dedicated low-mid vocal warmth pocket; never thin, distant, karaoke-wet, or buried under the club bed. Keep vocals thick and humanized under dense club production (warm doubles, pristine high-end air boost).
5. **PRE-DROP TRIGGER:** The 1–2 bars before the final drop must culminate in a sharp actionable command or one emotionally heavy phrase — production trigger point (*"Run," "Now," "Just look at me"*).
6. **LAYOUT INTEGRATION:** Sparse text only in breakdown/build-up/outro markers. Leave air for kicks, subs, and filter sweeps.
7. **NO PRODUCTION-AS-EMOTION:** Never name *beat, drop, bass, floor, lights* as the thing that saves or heals the singer.

## SUB-GENRE MATRIX

### 1. BIG ROOM TECHNO / PEAK-TIME (`peak_time_techno`)

**Auto-select when:** Big Room Techno · Peak-Time Techno · Hard Techno · Minimal Techno · Acid Techno · Schranz · Industrial Techno · generic Techno (non-melodic).

- **Vibe/Focus:** Internal monologues, physical boundaries, raw intimacy, sensory overload — psychological pressure over narrative exposition.
- **Arrangement:** Spoken-word or whispered delivery; stark contrast against aggressive driving kicks.
- **Syllables:** **MELODY-SYNC** TECHNO / HOUSE / LOOP GRIDS row; Pre-Drop trigger 2–5 syllables.

### 2. UPLIFTING / VOCAL TRANCE (`vocal_trance`)

**Auto-select when:** Uplifting Trance · Progressive Trance · Vocal Trance · Trance · Festival EDM · Big Room EDM · Mainstage Dance.

- **Vibe/Focus:** Longing, unrequited love, turning points, emotional release — anchored in **felt honesty**, not galaxy/neon poetry or cinematic travelogue.
- **Arrangement:** Floating harmonies, long-held vowels, highly repetitive hooks chop-ready during build-up.
- **Syllables:** **MELODY-SYNC** FESTIVAL ANTHEMS row.

### 3. DEEP HOUSE / MELODIC TECHNO (`deep_melodic`)

**Auto-select when:** Deep House · Soulful House · Melodic Techno · Melodic House · Progressive House · Tech House · generic House (non-big-room).

- **Vibe/Focus:** Conversational fragments, casual intimacy, relationship tension, cynicism mixed with hope — cool, detached, intimate psychological realism.
- **Arrangement:** Low-register dry vocals — spoken or softly sung tight to the capsule.
- **Syllables:** **MELODY-SYNC** TECHNO / HOUSE / LOOP GRIDS + Build fragments 2–4 syllables.
