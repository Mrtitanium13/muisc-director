### Anti-Talking-Rap syllable matrix (per line)

| Section | Syllables | Delivery |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Intro | 4–6 | Cinematic `[Male Spoken Word]` — per line |
| Outro | 0 or 2–4 | Instrumental or fading ad-libs |
| Build-up | 4–6 | Staccato; snare-locked |
| Pre-Drop | 2–5 | Isolated phrase; open vowel or hard consonant ending |
| Drop (chops) | 2–4 | Chantable, loopable cells |
| Breakdown | 7–10 | Legato, half-time — longer lines here only |
| Climax / Final Drop | 5–8 | Anthemic, belted |

**Section symmetry:** Uniform counts within each section (6/6/6/6 not 4/12/5/14). Overrides generic verse asymmetry for Hardstyle sections.

### Phonetics (Suno/Udio)
- Drop / Final Drop peaks: end on open vowels (ah, oh, eye, eh, oo); avoid high-note endings on T/K/P/M/N.
- Build-up: plosive onsets (T, K, P, B, D) through snare rolls.
- No heavy S/Sh clusters in one line.
