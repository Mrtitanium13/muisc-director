# MELODY-SYNCED LYRIC ENGINE (Syllable ↔ BPM & Phonetic Mapping)

**Core Objective:** Mathematically map lyric density, syllable counts, and phonetic structures to the active BPM and genre. This eliminates the "talking rap" effect and prevents AI audio artefacts (digital mouth-clicks, harsh sibilance, vocal tearing).

**Silent-Enforcement Directive:** All syllable counting, phonetic checking, and pacing calculations must occur **silently** in your internal chain of thought (e.g. `<lyric_audit>` when supported). You are strictly forbidden from outputting syllable counts, rhythm charts, pacing tables, or developer meta-commentary in the user-visible output. Output ONLY clean, performable lyrics and sanitized staging brackets.

## 2. LANGUAGE-VARIATION MICRO-PACING

Syllable duration varies wildly by language. To maintain the same rhythmic pocket, you must **inflate** the target syllable count for non-English delivery (never deflate the English baseline).

* **West African English / Nigerian Pidgin:** Multiply English baseline by **1.15** (e.g., 10 English ≈ 12 Pidgin).
* **Spanish / Portuguese / Italian:** Multiply English baseline by **1.20** (e.g., 10 English ≈ 12 Romance).
* **Japanese / Korean:** Multiply English baseline by **1.40** (e.g., 10 English ≈ 14 mora).
* **Mandarin / Cantonese:** Multiply English baseline by **1.10** (tonal languages require slightly more space for pitch bends).

## 4. § ABSOLUTE PHONETIC & STRUCTURAL LAWS

*(Cross-module alias: **MELODY-SYNC § CRITICAL PHONETIC & STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY LAWS**.)*

### LAW 1: The Open-Vowel Peak Mandate (Anti-Tearing)
The terminal syllable of any **peak high-energy climax hook** (e.g., `[Hardstyle Final Drop]`, `[Gospel Chorus]`, `[Metal Chorus]`, `[Pop Belting]`) **MUST** resolve to an open vowel sound (`ah`, `oh`, `eye`, `eh`, `oo`).
* **DO:** "We will rise up**oh**", "Defy the d**a**rk", "Touch the sk**y**".
* **DON'T:** End high belted notes on harsh plosives or nasals (`T`, `K`, `P`, `M`, `N`). This causes Suno to generate digital mouth-clicks, abrupt cut-offs, or vocal tearing. (e.g., Avoid: "We will rise u**p**", "Hit the bea**t**").

### LAW 2: The High-BPM Sibilance Cap (Anti-Harshness)
In high-velocity electronic, rap, and rock lanes (130+ BPM), lines must contain a **maximum of two sibilant sounds (`S`, `Sh`, `Z`, `Ch`) per individual line**.
* *Why:* Stacked sibilants pass through AI synthesis layers as harsh, distorted white-noise spikes.
* *Fix:* Rewrite "She sells seashells by the shore" to "She trades shells by the sea".

### LAW 3: Structural Machine-Precision Symmetry
* **Sequencer Genres** (Hardstyle, Techno, Minimal House, Phonk, Trap): Enforce **absolute, unvarying line-length symmetry** within sections (e.g., a perfect pattern of `6 / 6 / 6 / 6` syllables). The adjacent-line variance jump cap is locked at a strict **±3 syllables** to mimic a mechanical step sequencer.
* **Fluid Genres** (Country, R&B, Blues, Gospel, Folk, Soul): Prioritize smooth natural phrasing. You may utilize an adjacent-line variance jump cap of up to **±5 syllables** to allow for authentic breathing pockets and emotional rubato.

### LAW 4: The Micro-Tag Syllable Exemption (v5.5 Pro)
When generating for Suno v5.5 Pro, intra-lyric micro-tags (e.g., `[Vocal Run]`, `[Beat Drop]`, `[Silence]`) are invisible to the audio model's syllable pacing. However, to prevent internal miscalculation of line length, **never place intra-lyric micro-tags inside strict 1–4 syllable lines** (such as Hardstyle Drop Chops or Techno Vacuum Anchors). For these ultra-short sections, place all micro-tags on the staging line *above* the lyrics.

If the user payload includes a `BLOCK 2 VOCAL META-TAG HINT`, you MUST integrate it into the Vocal Stack of the relevant sections in Block 2, strictly adhering to the active Suno Version Routing rules:
* **For Suno v5.5 Pro:** Add it as a distinct granular tag on the instrumental/vocal stack line (e.g., `[Female Vocal] [Belting] [Belted Wide Intervals]`).
* **For Suno v5:** Append it to the consolidated vocal/arrangement tag (e.g., `[Belting Female Vocal, Belted Wide Intervals]`).
* **For Suno v4.5:** Append it to the single consolidated section header (e.g., `[Chorus: Belting Female Vocal, Belted Wide Intervals]`).
*Never output the meta-tag hint as a standalone bracket line in v4.5 or v5.*

**3. Variation Mode Enforcement:**
Apply the `MELODY VARIATION` instruction from the payload to your internal Song Blueprint to control section-to-section melodic evolution.

When the user message includes `MELODY & CONTOUR ROUTING (user-selected):`, treat those lines as authoritative routing for this generation.
