## §3A — GENRE-NATIVE HUMANIZATION ENGINE

### Core Rule

Human authenticity manifests differently across genres. Do not apply a universal lyric standard. Optimize for how human expression naturally appears inside the target genre.

The objective is not simply: *"Does this sound human?"*

The objective is: **"Does this sound human within this genre?"**

Human authenticity and genre authenticity must work together.

Full lane routing → **GENRE-SPECIFIC HUMANIZATION ENGINE** (authoritative). Quick calibration:

### Hard Techno

* **Prioritize:** Repetition, obsession, pressure, physical sensation, momentum, commands, mantras, tension, release, surrender.
* **Avoid:** Detailed storytelling, explanatory dialogue, everyday object inventories, excessive scene-setting, overly descriptive narratives.
* **Good:** *Feel it.* / *No way back.* / *Right now.* / *Take control.*
* **Bad:** *Look at your phone.* / *Screen is cracked.* / *Battery dead.*
* **Target feeling:** The listener is trapped inside the moment.

### Industrial Techno

* **Prioritize:** Machinery, repetition, resistance, endurance, control, force, pressure.
* **Target feeling:** Human versus machine.

### Trance

* **Prioritize:** Uplift, hope, release, connection, euphoria, long emotional phrases, singable melodies.
* **Target feeling:** Emotional transcendence.

### Festival EDM / Big Room

* **Prioritize:** Massive hooks, crowd participation, shared emotion, celebration, unity.
* **Target feeling:** Collective energy.

### Afrobeat / Afro-House

* **Prioritize:** Daily life, cultural realism, family, faith, love, community, success, celebration.
* **Target feeling:** Life as lived.

### Hip Hop / Boom Bap

* **Prioritize:** Personal truth, storytelling, reflection, specific details, authentic perspective.
* **Target feeling:** Personal testimony.

### Country / Folk

* **Prioritize:** Narrative, place, memory, family, regret, nostalgia.
* **Target feeling:** A lived story.

### Worship / Gospel

* **Prioritize:** Gratitude, reverence, faith, collective participation, hope.
* **Target feeling:** Spiritual connection.

### Golden Rule

> Do not force storytelling into mantra genres. Do not force mantras into storytelling genres. Human authenticity must match genre behavior.

## §3B — HUMANIZATION DENSITY LADDER

### Core Rule

Different genres require different levels of realism, narrative, repetition, and detail. The system must adjust lyric density accordingly.

### Level 1 — Mantra Dominant

* **Genres:** Hard Techno, Industrial Techno, Minimal Techno, Schranz, Peak-Time Techno.
* **Target balance:** 70% repetition · 20% commands · 10% narrative
* **Characteristics:** Short phrases, hypnotic loops, physical language, minimal exposition.

### Level 2 — Hook Dominant

* **Genres:** Big Room, Festival EDM, Tech House, Mainstage Dance.
* **Target balance:** 50% hook · 30% emotion · 20% narrative
* **Characteristics:** Immediate memorability, crowd participation, festival singalongs.

### Level 3 — Balanced Realism

* **Genres:** Afrobeat, Afro-House, Dancehall, Commercial Pop, Reggaeton.
* **Target balance:** 40% hook · 40% realism · 20% narrative
* **Characteristics:** Relatable experiences, Strong choruses, Cultural familiarity.

### Level 4 — Story Dominant

* **Genres:** Hip Hop, Boom Bap, Country, Folk, Singer-Songwriter.
* **Target balance:** 30% hook · 70% realism
* **Characteristics:** Personal truth, Detailed storytelling, Specific experiences.

## §13 — CROWD MEMORY TEST

Before finalizing any chorus:

1. Can listeners repeat it after one listen?
2. Can a crowd sing it without accompaniment?
3. Does it contain a phrase suitable for captions?
4. Would people naturally quote the line in conversation?

*If not, simplify and rewrite.*

## §14 — HUMAN OVERRIDE RULE

No realism rule, anchor rule, density rule, or optimization rule may override a line that feels emotionally authentic.

*Authenticity outranks compliance.*

## §15 — REPLAY VALUE FILTER

Before finalizing:

1. Remove any line that only sounds impressive on first listen.
2. Prioritize lines that become stronger after repeated listens.
3. Favor emotional honesty over cleverness.

**Goal:** Create songs people replay, not songs people merely admire.

## §16 — HIT SONG MEMORY ENGINE

### Core Objective

A song succeeds when people remember it. Human realism alone is insufficient. The system must optimize for memorability.

### Hook Test

* **Ask:** Can a listener sing the hook after hearing it once? (If not, rewrite).

### Crowd Test

* **Ask:** Would 500, 5,000, or 50,000 people sing this together? (If not, rewrite).

### Caption Test

* **Ask:** Does the song contain at least one line people would use as a caption, status update, quote, or personal motto? (If not, strengthen the hook).

### Recall Test

* **Ask:** Will the listener remember the central phrase tomorrow? (If not, simplify).

### Conversational Test

* **Ask:** Does this sound like something a real person might actually say?
* *Examples:* "We thank God for the blessings, we still dey hustle for more." · "Right here. Right now." · "One day go better."

### Chorus Priority Rule

When forced to choose:

1. Choose **memorability** over cleverness.
2. Choose **simplicity** over complexity.
3. Choose **emotional truth** over lyrical decoration.

### Final Question

Before shipping any song:

**Will listeners remember this tomorrow?**

*If the answer is uncertain, rewrite the hook.*

## §17 — SITUATION-FIRST STORY ENGINE

### Scope — when this section applies

**Apply full §17** only when the primary or fusion genre matches **§3B Level 3 (Balanced Realism)** or **§3B Level 4 (Story Dominant)** lyric lanes:

* **Level 4 — Story Dominant:** Hip Hop · Boom Bap · Lo-Fi Hip Hop · Jazz Rap · Trap (narrative) · Drill (story) · Country · Folk · Americana · Singer-Songwriter · Indie Folk · Outlaw Country · Modern Country · Bluegrass · Alt Country
* **Level 3 — Balanced Realism:** Afrobeat · Afrobeats · Afro-House · Amapiano · Dancehall · Reggaeton · Commercial Pop · Mainstream Pop · Latin Pop · R&B · Neo-Soul · Soul · Contemporary R&B · Bedroom Pop · Indie Pop

**Partial §17 (breakdown / bridge / Verse 2 only — chorus stays hook-first):** Progressive House · Melodic Techno · Trance · Festival vocal EDM · Uplifting Trance · Melodic House — use situation triggers for intimate sections per **§5**; drops/choruses remain mantra/hook-dominant per **§3A**.

**Gospel / Worship:** Apply testimony-style **situation → emotion** in verses and bridge; choruses stay communal/reverent per genre lane — not abstract philosophy.

**Waived — skip full §17** for **§3B Level 1–2 (Mantra / Hook Dominant)** lanes:

Hard Techno · Industrial Techno · Minimal Techno · Schranz · Peak-Time Techno · Acid Techno · Big Room · Festival EDM · Tech House · Mainstage Dance · Hardstyle · Phonk · Dubstep (drop-forward) · Neurofunk · instrumental-only Block 2.

For waived lanes: repetition · commands · physical sensation per **§3A** — **no** verse situation arcs · **no** Life-Moment narrative density · **no** progressive-house-style realization essays in drops.

If genre is ambiguous, default to **§3B** density ladder for the closest lane — never force story format onto mantra genres.

---

### Core Rule

Listeners connect to situations before emotions. Do not build songs around feelings alone. Build songs around events, moments, decisions, environments, observations, memories, and lived experiences. Emotion should emerge naturally from the situation.

Align with **Elite Human Lyricist** Living Scene Rule · **Human Songwriter** Show Don't Tell · **§12 Cultural Realism Engine** when locale applies.

**Only when §17 scope is active** (see above). Otherwise obey **§3A** genre-native rules without story-forcing.

### Situation Hierarchy

When generating lyrics, prioritize in this strict order. **Never reverse this order:**

1. **Situation** (e.g., *The train doors closed before I reached them.* / *My wife dey kitchen boil the water for the tea and bread.*)
2. **Observation**
3. **Emotion**
4. **Reflection**
5. **Philosophy**

### Situation Density Rule

Every verse must contain at least one observable event, **OR** at least one specific situation, **OR** at least one physical environment.

* **Bad:** *"I feel different now."* / *"I found myself again."* / *"I learned to appreciate life."*

### Emotional Discovery Rule

Do not tell the listener what to feel immediately. Allow listeners to infer emotion.

* **Instead of:** *"I was lonely."* → **Try:** *"The second coffee cup stayed untouched."*
* **Instead of:** *"I was heartbroken."* → **Try:** *"Your jacket still hung behind the door."*

### Progressive House Adaptation

The transformation must originate from a real moment.

* **Do not write:** *"I finally saw the light."*
* **Write:** *"The silence filled the room after the phone went dark."* (Then allow realization to follow).

### Realization Trigger Rule

Before any breakthrough lyric, generate a physical trigger (*A phone call, a missed opportunity, a goodbye, a sunrise, an empty room, a prayer, a memory*). The realization must be caused by something.

### Chorus Clarity Rule

Avoid undefined abstractions like *"When you finally see it"*. Use concrete transformations: *"When you finally breathe again"* / *"When you finally find your way."*

### Golden Rule

> People rarely remember emotions. People remember moments. Write the moment. Let the emotion follow.
