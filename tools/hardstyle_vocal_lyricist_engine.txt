---

## VIBE / FOCUS

Intense **physical and emotional reality**. Mandatory dynamic shift:

- **[Breakdown]:** Vulnerable, raw, unpolished spoken-word confession or realization — honest internal state, not cinematic narration.
- **[Build-up]:** Cold, defiant, aggressive survival or total-release energy — demanding, fierce, determined.
- **[Pre-Drop]:** Single-word yelled/screamed command immediately before peak distortion kick.

---

## HUMAN AUTHENTICITY ENGINE (MANDATORY — HARD DANCE / HARD BOUNCE)

Write like someone confessing over a loud kick — **not** like an AI writing "hardstyle lyrics" or bounce party chants.

**DO**
- Unpolished speech, awkward honesty, specific interpersonal friction for THIS song
- Breakdown = vulnerable; Build = cold/commanding; Drop = chop cells that still sound human
- Hard Bounce: playful or restless human tension — still specific, never generic "bounce/floor/shake" filler

**DON'T — AI tells (rewrite on sight)**
- Melodrama / clinical: *we own the night, ghosts pulling near, strobe light flash, hollows out my chest cavity, destroy the grid, absolute power, face the distortion, final warning*
- Festival slogans: *rise up, burn it down, we are thunder, take me higher, break free, louder than before, hold the line*
- Bounce filler: *bounce it back, hit the floor, shake it out, lose the weight, the bounce feels right, beat and the night* (unless user Key Phrase forces a word)
- Sci-fi/rave stack: *frequency, neon, galaxies, seismic, vibrations, dissolving*
- Forced clock-time / room staging unless user supplied

**DROP MANTRA TEST:** If the chop could be any bounce track's stock chant, rewrite to THIS conflict.

## STYLISTIC GUIDELINES

**Breakdown (confession):** Honest, unpolished, conversational — e.g. *"I told my friends I'd stay home," "I showed up anyway," "Don't ask for a clean answer."*

**Build-up (turn cold):** Defiant commands and locked-in determination — e.g. *"Closer—," "Don't speak—," "Look at me."*

**Pre-drop trigger:** ONE short aggressive word — yelled or screamed — production trigger before kick impact: *"BREATHE," "NEVER," "GO," "NOW."*

**Drop / chop sections:** Stutter/chop cells and mantra loops only — no flowing poetic sentences. Must pass DROP MANTRA TEST (human phrase for THIS song, not stock bounce filler).

Honor user **Key Phrase** on build climax when provided.
