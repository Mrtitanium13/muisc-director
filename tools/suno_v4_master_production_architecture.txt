### LAYER 3: REGIONAL & GENRE AUTHENTICITY MATRIX (COMPLETE TAXONOMY MATCH)

**Authority:** **GENRE-SPECIFIC HUMANIZATION ENGINE**. Map structural density, lyric length, and physical imagery vocabulary strictly to the active subset from `GenreData`.

#### §3.1 — ELECTRONIC DANCE MUSIC (EDM)
* **Techno / Hard Techno / Acid / Big Room Techno:** Level 1 Mantra Dominant (70% repetition). Maximum 2–4 words per line. Prioritize sensory overload, industrial warehouse space, and relentless machine momentum. *Banned:* Linear romantic narratives, everyday object lists.
* **Deep House / Tech House / Soulful / Progressive / Melodic / Future House:** Level 2/3 Balanced Realism. Deep House requires intimate, soulful U87 style close-mic vocals, Rhodes/Juno textures, and tight sidechain pump. Tech House requires minimal, spoken word vocals and rolling grooves. Progressive/Future House requires massive festival-scale anthemic builds and high-energy crowd sing-alongs.
* **Vinahouse / Amapiano-Vinahouse:** Offbeat bounce grooves, pentatonic *đàn tranh* hooks, and traditional Vietnamese melodic accents. For the Amapiano hybrid, use breathy close vocals over a log-drum sub pocket.
* **Dubstep / Melodic Dubstep / Future Bass / DnB / Liquid DnB:** Half-time rhythmic grids (140 BPM half-time for Dubstep) vs fast breakbeat chops (160–175 BPM for DnB). Focus on high-energy adrenaline rushes, intense vocal chop leads, or airy reverb-drenched vocal washes for melodic lanes.
* **Hardstyle / Rawstyle / Hard Bounce / EDM Bounce:** Spoken manifestos followed by long silences `...`, massive pitch lifts, and single-syllable pre-kick anchors hitting right before a punishing distorted bass climax.
* **Nu-Disco / Future Funk / Disco House / UK Garage / Jersey Club:** Shuffled, swung syncopated rhythms. Use 2-step garage chops, LinnDrum pocket aesthetics, and short, chopped vocal stabs.

#### §3.2 — URBAN, POP, & SOUL
* **Hip Hop / Boom Bap / Drill (UK & NY) / Phonk:** Level 4 Story Dominant (70% Realism). Max 4–5 SPB. Boom Bap requires gritty cratedigger breaks and street-corner observations. Drill requires sliding 808s, tense minor keys, and localized neighborhood slang. Phonk requires distorted Memphis cowbells and aggressive, lo-fi vocal pockets.
* **Lo-Fi Hip Hop / Chillhop / Cloud Rap / Jazz Rap:** Relaxed, hazy environments. Swung pocket grids, vinyl surface noise, Rhodes tape saturation, and languid, atmospheric vocal deliveries.
* **R&B / Contemporary / Soul / Neo-Soul / New Jack Swing / Quiet Storm / Trap Soul:** Stacked vocal harmonies, late-night vulnerable confessions, and close-mic proximity. Neo-Soul focuses on organic, behind-the-beat grooves. Quiet Storm uses long plate reverbs and smooth late-night instrumentation.
* **Pop (Mainstream, Max Martin, Electropop, Dance, Bedroom, Indie, Hyperpop):** Pop uses pristine radio hooks and strict structural dynamics. *Pop / Max Martin rules:* Absolute melodic symmetry (A-A-B-A structural phrasing), percussive verses, legato chorus vowels, and fresh ear-candy switches every 8 seconds. Hyperpop mandates chaotic detuned synthesizers and heavily bit-crushed vocal processing.
* **K-Pop / J-Pop / C-Pop / Mandopop / City Pop:** Group vocal stacks, rapid multi-lingual cadence shifts, and dramatic pre-chorus builds. City Pop requires signature 80s Tokyo funk guitars, DX7 e-pianos, and breezy urban lifestyle imagery.

#### §3.3 — ROCK, METAL, & ALTERNATIVE
* **Rock / Indie / Alt / Pop Punk / Emo / Punk / Hard Rock / Classic Rock:** Band-forward mixing setups. Raw vocal grit, aggressive kit rooms, and stadium-sized choruses. Pop Punk and Emo require rapid, high-energy emotional delivery.
* **Metal / Heavy Metal / Metalcore / Death Metal:** High-intensity vocal styling parameters (screams, growls, aggressive rhythmic grunts). Machine-gun double bass patterns, detuned raw guitars, and high-velocity tempos (120–250 BPM).
* **Post-Rock / Shoegaze / Dream Pop:** Wall-of-sound textures. Shimmer reverb washes, reverse guitar delays, and breathy, indistinct vocals buried beautifully inside a massive room-mic drum mix.

#### §3.4 — REGIONAL, ROOTS, & GLOBAL TRADITIONS
* **Country / Modern / Outlaw / Americana / Bluegrass / Folk / Singer-Songwriter:** Level 4 Story Dominant. Linear character timelines, specific geographical landmarks, family heirlooms, manual labor, and acoustic porch intimacy. Twang-forward vocal tracking.
* **Gospel / Traditional / Praise & Worship / Modern / CCM / Afro-Gospel:** Community or studio isolation modes. Faith-first lyrical testimonies. Ban all secular club terminology or electronic descriptors (`supersaw`, `filter sweep`). Use traditional worship cues: Hammond swells, multi-tracked vocal overlays, or call-and-response structures.
* **Jazz / Vocal Jazz / Bebop / Smooth / Fusion / Nu-Jazz / Acid Jazz / Blues:** Smoky room character, loose behind-the-beat micro-timing, brass ensemble stabs, and upright acoustic bass dynamics. Blues requires a raw, vintage live-room vibe.
* **Latin (Reggaeton, Dembow, Bachata, Salsa, Bossa Nova, Cumbia, Brazilian Funk, Sertanejo):** Precise syncopated rhythm grids. Reggaeton and Dembow require the syncopated 3+3+2 dembow kick-snare grid over clean 808 sub pockets. Bachata mandates intricate *requinto* nylon guitar picking and traditional bongo/güira interplay.
* **Reggae / Roots / Dub / Dancehall / Soca:** Heavy dub delays, deep bass guitar loops, and rhythmic offbeat skank patterns. Dancehall and Soca switch to sharp syncopated up-tempo grooves and high-energy party ad-libs.
* **Afro/World (Afrobeats, Highlife, Fuji, Gqom, Amapiano, Bollywood, Punjabi, Bhangra):** Afrobeats demands melody-driven swagger, Pidgin inflections, and bright shaker loops. Amapiano is strictly anchored by synthetic FM log-drums and repetitive lifestyle chant patterns. Gqom strips back to dark, heavy, minimal club percussion sweeps.
* **Cinematic / Film Score / Orchestral / Trailer / Ambient / Synthwave:** Wide dynamic scoring ranges (40–180 BPM). Granular synthesizer layers, lush convolution reverbs, or massive brass sections. Synthwave/Retrowave requires retro 80s gated snare profiles and driving synth basslines.
