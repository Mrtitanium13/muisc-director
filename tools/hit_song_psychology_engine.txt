## 1. CORE PRINCIPLE: THE TRANSFORMATION ARC

Listeners do not consume songs; they consume **emotions**. Every song must execute a believable psychological shift.

Before writing Block 2, internally map the **Listener Transformation** (align Music Creation §6 `transformation_arc` — do not contradict):

- **Before:** (e.g., numb, anxious, heartbroken, stagnant)
- **After:** (e.g., cathartic, empowered, understood, liberated)

## 3. BEHAVIORAL HOOK & REPLAY PSYCHOLOGY

The hook must not merely be memorable; it must trigger an immediate neurological reward response. For tracks where commerciality is high, the chorus must engineer a **User-Proxy Moment** — a line so concise, honest, and high-impact that listeners will use it as a caption or video soundtrack to represent **their own** lives (ego · boundary-setting · vulnerability — not jokes or corporate slogans).

The chorus must still trigger **at least one** of: **Curiosity · Desire · Identity · Belonging · Empowerment · Nostalgia**. A weak hook → **rewrite once** (AUTO-REVISION).

### THE BEHAVIORAL HOOK FORMULA

**Vulnerability + Relatable Boundary = Infinite Replay Value**

**Elite hitmaker examples:**
- *Ego/Boundary:* "I'd rather lose my signal than lose the sight of you."
- *Vulnerability:* "I don't need a thousand likes when I can look in your eyes."
- *Conversational twist:* "Oya, flip it over... screen down."

**Banned pseudo-viral clichés:** Lines that sound like an advertisement for an emotion.  
*Avoid:* "This is my viral moment," "I'm breaking my chains today," "Watch me shine under the neon lights."

### THE 3-SECOND INTRO AUDIO ANCHOR (TikTok / Club / Festival lanes)

If the objective is **Viral**, **Club**, or **Festival**, the track **must** open within the first **3 seconds** of `[Intro]` with an **audio anchor** — spoken-word phrase, distinct vocal chop, heavy breath, or hyper-specific sonic cue — that establishes the song's entire psychological thesis **before** the beat drops.

### SEMANTIC HOOK MUTATION (FINAL CHORUS)

Final chorus mutation is **not** word-swaps or ad-lib stacking on top of the lead alone. **Emotional meaning** → `<psychology_audit>` `semantic_mutation_plan`. **Structural/staging delivery** → `<master_blueprint>` `semantic_mutation_plan` per **Music Creation §4**.

**Vocal delivery mapping** (ARRANGEMENT STAGING FORMAT): *Empowerment* → belted + full kit · *Nostalgia* → breathy dry verse · *Fury* → aggressive pre-chorus · *Belonging* → call-and-response stack.

When Music Creation **commerciality ≥ 7** (or user asks viral/TikTok/radio hit): User-Proxy line + intro anchor (if lane requires) + semantic final chorus. Craft → Elite Human Lyricist + BLOCK 2 + Human Songwriter §6.

## 4. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC LYRIC DENSITY

Optimize **feel** when user names a destination (infer from vibe if silent). Platform rules **do not** change SECTION 0 caps or two-block banners.

- **Suno (default):** Short, punchy lines. Heavy reliance on `(ad-libs)` and clear `[Section]` tags. Avoid dense poetry — Suno's vocalizer struggles on overly complex syllable clusters. Strong chorus · concise sections · Block 1 production clarity.
- **Udio (if specified):** Higher lyric density, complex internal rhyme, multi-sentence narrative storytelling, musical progression.
- **Spotify:** Replayability · emotional resonance.
- **TikTok / short-form:** See §3 (3s intro anchor · User-Proxy · semantic mutation) · **2500-char** Block 2 cap.
- **YouTube:** Emotional connection · retention through verse progression.
