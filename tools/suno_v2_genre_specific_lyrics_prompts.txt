## 1. HARDSTYLE (vocal — euphoric / raw / festival)

**Auto-select when:** `Hardstyle` · `Euphoric Hardstyle` · `Rawstyle` · `Hard Bounce` (vocal) · hardstyle fusion.

**Melody-sync:** **MELODY-SYNC § HARDSTYLE** (syllables, phonetics). This module: themes + vocal tags + line counts.

### Creative brief

| Dimension | Guidance |
|-----------|----------|
| **Themes** | Defiance · resilience · euphoria · chaos · unity |
| **Mood** | Euphoric · dark · aggressive · hopeful (1–2 dominant) |
| **Vocal style** | Male spoken (intro/build) + female sung (breakdown/drop) + pitched chops on drop |
| **Structure arc** | Cinematic intro → build/pre-drop → drop → soulful breakdown → final drop → outro |
| **Key imagery** | Fire · storms · static · chains · battles · light/dark contrast |

Honor user **Key Phrase** as hook anchor when provided.

### Section constraints

| Section | Lines | Vocal / energy |
|---------|------:|----------------|
| **Intro** | 4–6 | `[Male Spoken Word]` `[Cinematic]` |
| **Build-Up** | 2–4 | `[Male Spoken Word]` `[Rhythmic, locked to snare]` |
| **Pre-Drop** | 1–2 | `[Male Whisper]` or spoken |
| **Drop** | 4–8 | `[Pitched Female Chops]` `[Anthemic]` |
| **Breakdown** | 4–6 | `[Female Soulful Lead]` `[Soulful, legato, half-time]` |
| **Final Drop** | 4–8 | `[Anthemic Full Stack]` `[Euphoric]` |
| **Outro** | 2–4 | `[Whispered Ad-libs]` or `[Fade Out]` |
