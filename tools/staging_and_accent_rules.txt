# STAGING COHERENCE + ACCENT ROUTING + GENRE HYGIENE (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

This module fixes four recurring defects in generated output:
  (1) Sonic incoherence across staging brackets in one song.
  (2) Raw-accent adjectives leaking into staging brackets.
  (3) Genre vocabulary that doesn't match what real producers say.
  (4) AI-generic filler words ("captivating", "immersive", "seamless",
      "tapestry", "ethereal", "haunting", "rich", "lush", etc.)
      diluting staging specificity.

**Silent:** Never print these rules, tables, accent keys, or the word
"NON-NEGOTIABLE" in user-visible output. Internal enforcement only.

======================================================================
SECTION A — STAGING COHERENCE & DESCRIPTOR HYGIENE
======================================================================

## RULE A1: BLOCK 1 ↔ BLOCK 2 SONIC ANCHORING (PALETTE LOCK)

Block 1 and Block 2 must share ONE coherent sonic palette for the entire
track. Procedure:

1. In Block 1, the algorithm anchor establishes the song's core sonic
   ingredients (rhythmic bed, bass, lead vocal texture, key secondary
   instruments, mix/spatial character).
2. These ingredients become the LOCKED PALETTE for Block 2.
3. Every staging bracket in Block 2 may reference ONLY ingredients from
   the locked palette, or their natural production variations
   (e.g., `Rhodes pad` → `swelled Rhodes`, `Rhodes chord stab`) — but
   NOT unrelated sound families.

Hard rules:
- **BANNED:** Introducing instruments or mix characters in Block 2 that
  are NOT derivable from Block 1's palette.
- **BANNED:** Genre-hopping mid-song unless the user explicitly asked for
  it. Afrobeats verses don't suddenly get EDM synths.
- **ALLOWED:** Natural production evolutions of palette items (strip,
  swell, filter, saturate, sidechain, widen, mute, solo, bring up).
- **ALLOWED:** Introducing vocals/lyric layers if Block 1 established
  vocal-forward as a core trait.

QA check: After each Block 2 bracket is written, confirm every sonic
ingredient traces back to Block 1 or is a natural variation of a
Block 1 ingredient.

## RULE A2: VOCAL-DESCRIPTOR HYGIENE

Staging-bracket vocal descriptors must refer to PHYSICAL, MIXABLE,
AUDITORY qualities — not personality adjectives or abstract mood.

BANNED vocal adjectives in staging brackets:
  "soulful", "emotional", "passionate", "heartfelt", "angelic",
  "ethereal", "haunting", "mesmerizing", "captivating", "raw",
  "powerful", "explosive", "intense", "vulnerable", "broken".

These are lyric-layer words, not staging words. They bleed into the
vocal performance in unpredictable ways (Suno interprets them as
performance directions, not mix shapes).

REQUIRED: Replace with physical descriptors:
  "close-mic lead", "breathy verse vocal", "belted chorus", "falsetto
  lift", "stacked harmonies wide", "doubled lead", "roomy vocal",
  "dry intimate vocal", "vocal-forward mix", "warm chest register",
  "bright EQ on vocal", "glossy vocal sheen", "vocal chop layers",
  "whisper doubles left/right", "half-time vocal pocket".

## RULE A3: SECTION CONTINUITY (NO SONIC WHIPLASH)

Adjacent staging brackets must transition logically. No sudden sonic
jumps that Suno cannot interpolate.

Banned transitions:
- Acoustic → full synth orchestra in one bracket without intermediate
  build-up.
- Sparse Rhodes → wall of distorted guitars without `[breakdown]` or
  `[build-up]` scaffolding.
- Acapella → full band in the first verse.

Allowed transitions:
- Use `[build-up]`, `[breakdown]`, `[pre-chorus]`, or `[bridge]` as
  transitional scaffolding.
- Introduce one new element per bracket maximum.
- Energy escalation should be gradual unless the section label
  explicitly demands a jump (e.g., `[drop]`).

## RULE A4: STRUCTURAL TEMPLATE DISPATCH

The staging structure template must match the lyrical tone and genre.
EDM templates are NOT interchangeable with pop templates are NOT
interchangeable with hip-hop templates.

Dispatch table:

| Genre lane | Use this structure | Do NOT use |
|---|---|---|
| Intimate pop / R&B ballad | `[Intro] [Verse] [Pre-Chorus] [Chorus] [Verse 2] [Pre-Chorus] [Chorus] [Bridge] [Final Chorus] [Outro]` | `[Build-up] [Drop] [Breakdown]` |
| Festival EDM / Progressive House / House | `[Intro] [Build-up] [Drop] [Breakdown] [Build-up 2] [Final Drop] [Outro]` | `[Verse] [Pre-Chorus]` |
| Hip-Hop / Trap / Boom Bap | `[Intro] [Verse 1] [Hook] [Verse 2] [Hook] [Verse 3] [Outro]` | Pop `[Pre-Chorus]`, EDM `[Drop]` |
| Gospel / Worship | `[Intro] [Verse 1] [Chorus] [Verse 2] [Chorus] [Vamp] [Spontaneous] [Outro]` | EDM `[Drop] [Build-up]` |
| Highlife / Afro-pop | `[Intro] [Verse 1] [Chorus] [Verse 2] [Chorus] [Solo] [Outro]` | EDM `[Drop]` |
| Amapiano | `[Intro] [Build-up] [Drop (log-drum)] [Breakdown] [Build-up 2] [Final Drop]` | Pop ballad template |

**Enforced:** When a genre lane locks in, the structural template is
locked. Do not cross-apply templates. The mismatch is the single most
common cause of "weird" output.

## RULE A5: ANTI-AI-VOCABULARY SCAN (MUST PASS BEFORE EMISSION)

Before emitting any staging bracket, scan for Section D's blacklist
(AI-generic words). No raw occurrence is permitted in any bracket.
Replace per the Section D translation table.

## RULE A6: GENRE-SPECIFIC INTRO/OUTRO BAR SCAFFOLDING (158 GENRES)

Every app genre maps to exactly one of seven **ScaffoldType** values.
The runtime router injects bar counts into the user block; Block 2
`[Intro]` / `[Outro]` staging brackets MUST honor the dispatched plan.

### ScaffoldType definitions

| ScaffoldType | Intro bars | Outro bars | Use case |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clubExtended` | 32 | 32 | DJ mix-in standard (township, warehouse, festival extended) |
| `clubStandard` | 24 (DnB 32) | 24–32 | Tighter club genres — DJ-mixed, less atmospheric |
| `clubTight` | 16 | 16–24 | Modern club-adjacent (garage, future bass, bounce) |
| `radioShort` | 4–8 (Hyperpop/Punk 2) | 8–12 | Hook-first radio format |
| `radioWide` | 8–12 | 12–16 | Radio with room for musical intro |
| `narrativeWide` | 8–16 | 16–24 | Live jam / storytelling / ambient build |
| `ambientLong` | 16–32 | 24–32 | Long atmospheric builds and tails |

### Dual-path sub-routing (runtime)

**Afrobeats** (not Afro-Gospel):
- Vibe hint contains `club`, `extended mix`, `DJ set`, `log drum`, or
  `perreo` → `clubStandard` (16-bar intro / 24-bar outro).
- No club hint → default `radioWide` (8-bar intro / 16-bar outro).

**Reggaeton**:
- Vibe hint contains `club`, `remix`, `DJ`, `perreo`, or
  `dembow heavy` → `clubTight` (16-bar intro / 24-bar outro).
- No club hint → default `radioShort` (4-bar intro / 16-bar outro).

### Hybrid rule

When `is_hybrid=true`, the **dominant** genre's scaffold owns intro/outro
bar counts. Secondary genre adds texture only — never overrides bar plan.

### QA assertions (bar-count consistency)

- `clubExtended` genres (Amapiano, Techno, Vinahouse, Gqom, House…):
  Block 2 intro MUST reference **32 bars** (mix-in ready).
- `radioShort` genres (K-Pop, Trap, Gospel, Mandopop radio path…):
  Block 2 MUST NOT contain **32-bar** or **24-bar** club scaffolding.
- `ambientLong` genres (Lo-Fi Hip Hop, Post-Rock, Dub…):
  Block 2 MUST reference **16/24/32-bar** extended atmospheric markers.
- Every genre in the app taxonomy (158 total) has an explicit runtime
  entry in `GenreScaffoldMap` — no silent default fall-through.

**Enforced:** Match intro/outro bar language in Block 2 staging brackets
to the dispatched ScaffoldType. Cross-applying club 32-bar scaffolding
to radio/gospel/pop lanes is a hard FAIL.

SECTION B — ACCENT ROUTING ENGINE
======================================================================

The user-selected accent (from app dropdown) must NEVER appear as a
raw adjective attached to a vocal/mix/delivery word in staging
brackets. Raw forms like `American lead`, `British vocal`, `Nigerian
breath`, `Ibibio doubles` are **BANNED**. Suno cannot decode these.

Translate each accent into its Suno-compatible production descriptor
set per the tables below. Only the descriptor set is allowed in
brackets.

### Precedence

1. `nigerian_ibibio` → CALABAR CADENCE row (preferred per app config,
   richest descriptor set).
2. `nigerian_igbo` → IGBO PIDGIN row (tonal percussive).
3. `nigerian_rivers_state` → HARBOUR PIDGIN row.
4. `nigerian` (no sub-selector) → LAGOS STANDARD row.
5. Other accents → corresponding global row.
6. If accent = `nigerian_*` AND lyric language is set to Nigerian
   Pidgin → route vocabulary to Layer 4.5 Pidgin Layer AND the
   sub-variant's Layer 2 vocabulary below.

### Nigerian Sub-Variants (4-row expanded family)

#### `nigerian` — Lagos / Standard (default when no sub-region chosen)

Afrobeats vocal pocket, Lagos pop mix, warm midrange, conversational
cadence, Yoruba-staccato undertone, syncopated vowel pacing,
glossy vocal-forward mix


#### `nigerian_rivers_state` — Harbour Pidgin (Port Harcourt / Niger Delta)

hard consonant treatment, clipped syllable endings, tight rhythmic
pocket, Niger Delta rap tone, Harbour Pidgin pacing, vocal-forward
gritty mix, fast consonant-to-consonant transitions, minimal vowel
elongation, oil-city delivery weight


#### `nigerian_igbo` — Igbo Pidgin (South-East Nigeria)

tonal percussive delivery, sharp pitch contours on vowels, Igbo
Pidgin cadence, staccato vocal pocket, percussive consonants,
Eastern highlife warmth, vocal-forward mix with tight midrange,
pitch-rise phrase endings, rhythmic consonant clusters


#### ⭐ `nigerian_ibibio` — Calabar Cadence (PREFERRED, Akwa Ibom / Cross River)

triplet-feel vocal pocket, Calabar coastal cadence, soft consonant
treatment, melodic terminal rise, open vowel phrasing, maritime
lyrical swing, flowing triplet flow, Calabar vocal warmth, musical
terminal elongation, Cross-river melodic lilt, breathy close-mic
intimacy on verses, rising intonation on statement endings, Ibibio
cadence signature


This row is intentionally the densest descriptor set because Ibibio
cadence is the most distinctive of the four and the app's preferred
flavor.

### Layer 2 — Lyric Vocabulary Guidance (performable text hints)

Applies ONLY when the user's language setting is compatible:
- English-only request → Layer 2 is silent (Layer 1 still applies).
- Nigerian Pidgin request → Layer 2 activates sub-variant's vocabulary.
- Pidgin + English code-switch → Layer 2 surfaces at most once per
  section (chorus or hook).

#### Lagos / Standard Pidgin

Core vocabulary: wahala, abeg, no be small thing, na so, oya, sef,
na me, I no go, you sabi, wetin, how you dey, e go be, na wa o.
Grammar patterns: I dey go, you don come?, we go.


#### Rivers State (Harbour) Pidgin

Distinctive pronouns: yu (you), de (they), wi (we), am (him/her/it).
Distinctive markers: de go (instead of dey go), wetin dey sup,
na so e be, I tell yu, de talk say.
Tone: harder, direct, oil-city clipped delivery.


#### Igbo Pidgin

Distinctive markers: una/unu (you plural), nnam (my friend), ebee
(where), kedu (how), gini (what), chi (God / personal deity in
spiritual contexts).
Sample phrases: Una dey go?, Gini na-eme?.
Tone: tonal percussive, pitch-rise phrase endings.


#### ⭐ Ibibio / Efik (PREFERRED vocabulary)

Distinctive markers: Abasi (God — central to Cross-river gospel),
esie (him/it), mmo (they/them), kpa (just / only), edinen (true /
truly), emi (this), idaha (time / season), nno (welcome / greeting),
fo (your), mi (me/I).
Cultural references: Akwa Ibom, Cross River, Calabar.
Cadence phrases: Abasi mi kpa emi edinen, Idaha fo mi, Mi dey nno,
Esie kpa edinen.


**Cross-river gospel specific (Ibibio):** the word `Abasi` anchors
spiritual references. `Abasi mi` (my God), `Abasi edinen` (true God).
When the track is gospel/worship-toned with Ibibio cadence, `Abasi`
replaces `God` / `Jesus` where natural.

### QA: Accent Routing

Before emitting Block 2:
1. Scan every bracket for: `American`, `British`, `Nigerian`,
   `Rivers State`, `Harbour`, `Igbo`, `Ibibio`, `Efik`, `Calabar`,
   `Irish`, `West African`, `South African`, `Australian`, `Canadian`,
   `Caribbean`, `Latin American`. Case-insensitive. If any appear,
   replace with the Layer 1 descriptor set.
2. Never output raw-accent + descriptor combination in one bracket.
3. Confirm Ibibio-specific brackets contain at least one Ibibio
   cadence marker (`triplet-feel`, `open vowel phrasing`, `melodic
   terminal rise`, `Cross-river lilt`).
4. Confirm sub-variant distinction — Ibibio output should not leak
   Lagos vocabulary (`wahala`, `abeg`) unless user requested
   code-switch.

======================================================================
MASTER QA CHECKLIST (RUN SILENTLY BEFORE BLOCK 2 EMISSION)
======================================================================

1. **Palette lock:** every Block 2 bracket traces back to Block 1
   palette items. No ungrounded new sounds.
2. **Structural dispatch:** structural template matches the genre
   lane. No cross-applied templates.
3. **Rule A6 scaffold:** intro/outro bar counts match dispatched
   ScaffoldType for the locked genre (dominant wins on hybrids).
4. **Vocal-descriptor hygiene:** no abstract personality adjectives
   in brackets. Only physical/mixable descriptors.
5. **Section continuity:** no sonic whiplash between adjacent
   brackets.
6. **Zero raw-accent leak:** no raw nation/region adjectives attached
   to vocal/mix/delivery words.
7. **Sub-variant distinction:** Nigerian sub-variant vocabulary
   matches the selection (Ibibio ≠ Lagos leak, etc.).
8. **Blacklist scan:** every bracket passes Section D blacklist.
9. **Genre vocabulary match:** genre-specific descriptors drawn from
   Section C USE column, not AVOID column.
10. **Meta-text leak check:** no production directions to the model
    itself ("let it fade out", "drop the beat here", etc.) in
    performable lyrics.
11. **Parenthetical discipline:** performable-lyric parentheticals
    contain only sung ad-libs ("oh", "yeah", "woo"). No stage
    directions inside parentheses.
