## 1. CANONICAL STAGING LINE SHAPE

**Formula:** `[bar-count or section role, sonic element, sonic element, mix/energy cue]`

- **One bracket**, comma-separated, **≤120 characters**
- **Bar counts:** `16-bar DJ intro`, `8-bar snare build`
- **Sonic elements:** `TR-909 kick`, `808 sub`, `supersaw`, `log drum bassline`, `Rhodes piano`, `reverse bass`, `FM pluck`, `đàn tranh`
- **Mix/energy:** `low-pass sweep`, `sidechain pump`, `filter open`, `plate reverb tail`
- **No** prose paragraphs · **no** gear SKU lists · **no** artist names · **invent fresh** per song

**Example:** `[16-bar filtered kick intro, rising hats, low-pass sweep]`

### INSTRUMENTAL STAGING CONSTRAINT (v5.5 SPEC)

Section tags for **instrumental-only** lanes (`[Instrumental Break]`, `[Instrumental Interlude]`, `[Log Drum Break]`, `[Build]`, `[Drop]` when lyric-free) must **never** contain descriptive **verb phrases**, action imperatives, or full sentences inside the bracket body.

- **Banned openers / patterns:** `Feature...`, `Features...`, `Play a...`, `Playing...`, `Let the...`, `Adding...`, `Introduce...`, or any instruction that reads like prose directed at a musician.
- **Why:** Suno treats dense bracket text as a continuous mix layer; sentence-like staging triggers robotic spoken artifacts, vocalizes bracket words, or skips the break entirely.
- **Required shape:** One bracket · **comma-separated noun/texture tokens only** · ≤120 characters · mix-bus or processing types welcome for gospel/pop hybrid polish.

| Wrong (sentence / verb leak) | Right (dense engineering tag) |
|------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| `[Instrumental Break: Feature bright strummed rhythm, tight pocket Acoustic Guitar, wide stereo, glossy pop sheen]` | `[Instrumental Break: Strummed Acoustic Arpeggios, Tight Rhythmic Pocket, Stereo Expansion, Glossy Pop Saturation, Dynamic Lift]` |
| `[Instrumental: Play a warm piano solo with reverb]` | `[Instrumental: Warm Piano Solo, Hall Reverb Tail, Stereo Width]` |

**v5.5 instrumental break stack:** Section header on its own line → **one** instrumental staging bracket (dense tokens) → **no** lyric paragraphs (≤3-word sung ad-libs OK on separate lines).

**Fourth-wall note:** Texture nouns in **staging brackets** (e.g. `Strummed Acoustic Arpeggios`, `Live Drum Kit`) are valid — **Elite Human Lyricist Fourth-Wall Law** applies to **performable lyric lines only**, not these engineering tags.

### TRADITIONAL GOSPEL / PRAISE & WORSHIP ARCHITECTURE

When **primary genre + fusion** map to Gospel · Praise & Worship · CCM · Southern Gospel · Afro-Gospel (not Afrobeat club lanes), staging brackets must maintain **studio-clean worship sonic purity** — never inherit EDM/club/electronic mix vocabulary and never simulate live congregation/crowd ambience (**STUDIO-ISOLATION DIRECTIVE**).

**Banned in gospel staging:** `sidechain pump`, `sidechain slam`, `supersaw`, `filter sweep`, `low-pass sweep`, `mix-out groove`, `DJ intro`, `DJ outro`, `16-bar filtered kick`, `riser`, `sub bloom`, `hat rolls`, `loop` (as club grid language), `EDM drop`, `club pocket` (unless Afro-Gospel lane explicitly allows Afro club hybrid — default Traditional Praise = **ban**).

**Authentic replacements:**

| Club / EDM leak | Gospel / sanctuary replacement |
|-----------------|--------------------------------|
| `sidechain pump` / `heavier sidechain` | `Analog VCA glue, warm dynamic leveling` |
| `DJ intro` / filtered kick intro | `Dead-room isolation, pristine studio environment, close-mic vocal tracking` |
| `DJ outro` / `mix-out groove` | `Sustained studio band resolution, clean multi-track fade, trailing organ decay` |
| `wide stacks` (generic pop) | `Isolated multi-tracked vocal doubles, Tight double-tracked vocal stacks` |
| `filter sweep` / `riser` | `natural room decay`, `warm Hammond swell`, `emotional dynamic dip` |

**Gospel lexicon priority:** Hammond B3 organ swell · tight studio drum backbeat · acoustic guitar strum · grand piano · tambourine lift · horn section stab · lush sustained studio strings · hall verb · praise-break groove · belted lead · isolated multi-tracked vocal doubles · multi-tracked vocal overlays.

**Cross-ref:** **GOSPEL / WORSHIP AUTHENTICITY ENGINE** · praise/worship drum matrix profile (no trap grids) · **STUDIO-ISOLATION DIRECTIVE** (below) overrides congregational/live crowd triggers in Intro/early tags.

### THE STUDIO-ISOLATION DIRECTIVE (ANTI-CROWD CHEERS)

**Default** when user `audio_environment_mode` is `studio_isolated` (or unset). **Fully overridden** by **LAYER 2.8: LIVE PERFORMANCE ARENA MODE** when user activates Live Performance Mode.

To prevent Suno from generating background crowd noise, live applause, or sanctuary ambient chatter at the start of the track, you must strictly enforce clean studio conditions:

1. **BAN LIVE EVENT TERMINOLOGY:** Completely forbid the words `Congregational`, `Live`, `Church`, `Sanctuary`, `Communal`, or `SATB Choir Stack` inside the `[Intro]` or early section tags.
2. **MANDATE COLD STUDIO TAGS:** The `[Intro]` must explicitly start with acoustic isolation and engineering cues. Always utilize phrases like: `[Dead-room isolation, Pristine studio environment, Zero audience noise, Close-mic vocal tracking]`.
3. **VOCAL LAYERING WORKAROUND:** Instead of asking for a `Choir` or `Congregation` which triggers live crowd noise, use isolated multi-tracking terms only: `[Isolated multi-tracked vocal doubles, Tight double-tracked vocal stacks, Multi-tracked vocal overlays]`. **Never** write `Studio Harmonic Backing`, `Harmonic Backing`, or `Harmonic Overlays` — Suno associates those with group gospel/room reflections.
4. **ANTI-TAPE-NOISE (INTRO / VERSE 1 / OUTRO):** Ban `tape hiss`, `subtle tape hiss`, `vinyl crackle`, and `room noise` in cold-open staging brackets — Suno may vocalize static at track start. Use pure acoustic spatial tags: `dry acoustic room`, `focused studio room`, `sparse fingerpicked acoustic guitar`, `dead-room silence`.

**Scope:** Intro · Verse 1 · Outro · all Chorus/Final Chorus brackets — **never** `SATB Choir Stack`, `congregational width`, `Harmonic Backing`, or `live-sanctuary` ambience tokens in any bracket.

**Gospel lane default:** Praise & Worship · CCM · Southern Gospel · Afro-Gospel staging = **studio-clean radio master**, not auditorium simulation. Hammond swell · warm analog organ · tight studio drum backbeat · SSL bus glue are valid; crowd/cheers/applause triggers are not.

### LAYER 2.8: LIVE PERFORMANCE ARENA MODE

When the **Live Performance Mode** feature is explicitly activated by the user (`audio_environment_mode=live_performance`), you must intentionally **override** the Studio-Isolation constraint and force Suno into a massive, ambient stadium setting.

#### MANDATORY LIVE SIMULATION LAWS:

1. **INTRO TRIGGER CODES:** The `[Intro]` block must strictly start with these crowd-inducing metadata tokens: `[Live concert ambience, Thunderous stadium crowd cheering, Loud audience applause, Large outdoor stage reverb, Massive venue air]`.
2. **SECTION SEPARATION CUES:** Inject crowd reaction cues directly inside the structural arrangement brackets to simulate true audience participation:
   - Change standard Chorus tags to: `[Anthemic Chorus, Crowd singing along loudly, Massive stadium sing-along response]`.
   - Change standard Bridge transitions to: `[Building intensity, Audience handclaps on the beat, Raw emotion]`.
3. **AD-LIB ENHANCEMENTS:** For the `[Outro]`, use trailing parameters: `[Sustained band resolution, Loud crowd screaming and cheering, Standing ovation, Natural stadium decay, Long applause]`.

**Precedence:** Live Arena Mode wins over Studio-Isolation · Traditional Gospel studio-clean defaults · and any anti-crowd sanitization when user flag is ON. Worship/gospel lanes may use thousand-voice choir swell and arena overlays — never dead-room isolation tokens.

## 3. DYNAMIC STAGING & LEXICON

**CRITICAL:** Repeated sections need **different** `{staging}` — identical chorus staging = identical instrumentals.

- *Chorus 1:* `[wide stacks, supersaw, sidechain pump, hook lift]`
- *Chorus 2:* `[wider stacks, counter-melody, heavier sidechain, bright EQ]`
