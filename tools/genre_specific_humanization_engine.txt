# AFROBEATS AUTHENTICITY ENGINE

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Afrobeats is driven by melody, localized lifestyle, rhythm, and effortless swagger. It requires conversational Pidgin-English inflections, cultural currency, and high-value romance or celebration.

## FOCUS AREAS

* **Increase:** Localized currency (Casamigos, Henny, G-Wagon, express traffic), rhythmic call-and-response hooks, syncopated line lengths that breathe with the 4/4 or 3/4 drum pocket, and physical/sensory assurance.
* **Reduce:** Complex metaphors, heavy emotional baggage, and standard Western pop clichés ("weeping souls," "shattered glass").
* **The Litmus Test:** Can the lyric slide smoothly between English and standard Nigerian Pidgin without sounding forced or grammatically rigid? If it sounds like a textbook, rewrite.

**Cross-refs:** **Human Songwriter §5** · **Elite Human Lyricist §2** fast/groove syllable weight (≤3 syllables on concrete anchors) · **MELODY-SYNC** mid-tempo pocket.

---

# AMAPIANO AUTHENTICITY ENGINE

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Amapiano is a club-centric, hypnotic groove where the vocal serves as a rhythmic instrument. It relies heavily on brief, chant-style hooks, crowd participation, and late-night premium atmosphere.

## FOCUS AREAS

* **Syllable law:** Strict **4–8 syllables per line max** during verses and choruses to respect the heavy log-drum pockets.
* **Increase:** Repeating audio anchors (hypnotic 1-to-2 bar loops), lifestyle flexes (tables at the lounge, champagne on ice), and crowd chants via `[Chant]` / `[Backing Vocal]` lines or bare sung parens `(yeah... yeah...)` — never `(Chant: ...)`, `(Harmony: ...)`, or `(Echo: ...)` (Elite §3).
* **Reduce:** Dense storytelling, multi-line narrative progression, and complex internal rhyme schemes.
* **Forbidden patterns:** Long explanatory sentences.  
  *Avoid:* "I am standing in the corner of the club thinking about our past relationship."  
  *Instead:* "In the corner of the section... eyes locked, no projection."

## AMAPIANO HYBRID PRODUCTION RULE (LAYER 3 — STAGING TAGS)

* **Log drum = synthesized FM bass:** Amapiano log drums are **digital FM club bass patches** (DX7-style), not live acoustic percussion. **Never** label them *live log drum*, *acoustic log drum*, or *live bass* in staging tags. Use *FM log drum groove*, *synthesized log drum bassline*, *club log drum pocket*, or *log drum-vocal interplay*.
* **Subordinate fusion accent (one-time only):** When Primary = Amapiano and Fusion adds a subordinate texture (e.g. jazz acoustic piano, indie guitar), name that subordinate element **once** as an **entry accent** in **Intro or Verse 1** only — e.g. `[Subordinate accent: sparse jazz piano stab, once]`. **Do not** repeat *acoustic*, *jazz piano*, or subordinate instrument names in Verse 2, Bridge, Instrumental Break, or later tags — those sections revert to dominant Amapiano staging (log drum, shaker, sub, club pocket) so Suno does not drift into an unpolished all-acoustic mix.
* **Bridge / interplay tags:** `[log drum and vocal interplay]` or `[FM bass-vocal call]` — never `[Live log drum...]`.

**Cross-refs:** **DUAL-GENRE HYBRIDIZATION LAW** · **SUNO V4 §1 Layer 3** · **Human Songwriter §1** · **Elite Human Lyricist §3** · runtime hybrid user block when fusion is set.

# AFRO-GOSPEL / AFRO-PRAISE AUTHENTICITY ENGINE

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Afro-Gospel is an explosive, high-energy fusion of divine gratitude, radical testimony, and infectious rhythms. It is inherently celebratory, communal, and deeply victorious.

## FOCUS AREAS

* **The joy filter:** It must **never** sound somber, defeated, or mournful. The lyric assumes the battle is already won.
* **Increase:** Declarations of unmerited favor, overcoming "the enemy" or bad seasons, communal call-and-response lines, and physical expressions of praise (dancing, clapping, shouting).
* **Cultural vocabulary:** Cross-regional expressions of gratitude and divine elevation (e.g., "Overwhelming grace," "He lifted me from the miry clay," "My cup is running over").
* **Congregational bounce:** The hook must be simple enough for an entire auditorium to catch and shout back after hearing it exactly once.

**Cross-refs:** **GOSPEL CHOIR AUTHENTICITY ENGINE** (below) for SATB/stacked praise · **Hit Song Psychology** belonging/empowerment triggers.

# MELODIC TECHNO AUTHENTICITY ENGINE

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Melodic Techno requires a cinematic, hypnotic, dark-pop or cyberpunk aesthetic (**122–126 BPM**). Vocals must serve as atmospheric elements or internal monologues drifting over rolling baselines.

## CADENCE & VOCAL STAGING

* **The Space Law:** Enforce a strict **Two-Line Max** per block. Every vocal phrase must be followed by long instrumental gaps so synth leads can breathe.
* **Structural Tags:** Strictly ban `[Pre-Chorus]`, `[Build-Up]`, and `[Drop]`. Replace with `[Atmospheric Progression]`, `[Cinematic Breakdown]`, `[Main Climax]`, and `[The Release]`.
* **Vocal Texturing:** Force processing directives like `[Reverb Wash]`, `[Pitched-Down Vocals]`, or `[Ethereal Delay Stacks]` into staging tags (square brackets on their own lines — **Elite Human Lyricist §3**).
* **The Audio Anchor Rule:** The climax must center on a singular, profound, existential phrase that acts as a mantra.

**Cross-refs:** **Music Creation §2** low/medium SPB · **PromptFlowData** era `Afterlife Melodic Techno vibe` · **MELODY-SYNC** · mood `Cyberpunk` / `Existential` when lane fits.

# HARDSTYLE AUTHENTICITY ENGINE

**Full Block 2 structure:** **GENRE-SPECIFIC LYRICS PROMPTS §1 HARDSTYLE**. **Syllable/rhythm law:** **MELODY-SYNC § HARDSTYLE** (150–160 BPM anti-talking-rap matrix).

## PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Hardstyle relies on theatrical, cinematic scale. It demands a stark contrast between dark, aggressive, high-stakes tension and ultimate, euphoric, stadium-sized liberation.

## CADENCE & VOCAL STAGING

* **The spoken intro/build:** Must read like a movie trailer or a warrior's manifesto. Short, punchy, declarative sentences with heavy pauses (`...`). No conversational clutter.
* **The sung breakdown:** High-register, soulful, soaring vocal toplines that stretch vowel sounds to create a massive wall of sound over the chords before the drum roll kicks in.
* **The drop anchor:** A singular, explosive, monosyllabic phrase or chant right before the kick impacts (e.g., *"Welcome to the chaos!"* or *"Break the chains!"*).
* **Forbidden patterns:** Any form of casual talking or laid-back rap phrasing. If a line cannot be delivered with cinematic intensity over a build or drop, rewrite or cut.

**Vocal lanes:** Male spoken word (intro/build) · Female sung (breakdown) · Pitched vocal chops (drop festival chant).

**Arc:** dark/weighted intro → hopeful breakdown → defiant release. Avoid generic club “put your hands up” without hardstyle imagery.

# DUAL-GENRE HYBRIDIZATION LAW (SPLIT-DNA ROUTING)

When **Primary genre + Fusion / sub-genre** are both set, route authenticity through **Genre A (dominant)** and **Genre B (subordinate)** — never arbitrary mashups.

1. **Rhythmic anchor (A):** BPM, drums, and structural tags (`[Atmospheric Progression]`, `[Main Climax]`, etc.) follow the **primary** lane engine (e.g. **MELODIC TECHNO AUTHENTICITY ENGINE** when A is Melodic Techno).
2. **Textural infusion (B):** B's instruments, vocal intimacy, and regional vocabulary appear **only** in stripped sections — Intro, Verse 1, Breakdown — not stacked on dominant build-ups.
3. **Hybridization drop rule:** At climax, B's acoustic/organic layers must be **processed** (sidechain, delay, low-pass, loop mutation) to sit on A's grid.
4. **Subordinate tag accent rule:** Genre B texture may appear **once** as an entry accent in Intro or Verse 1 staging — never as a **recurring** structural label across Verse 2, Bridge, or breaks (avoids tag creep that pulls Suno off the dominant electronic baseline).

**Example:** Melodic Techno + Indie Acoustic → 124 BPM techno structure; dry guitar in Verse/Breakdown; sidechained guitar texture at **Main Climax**. **Amapiano + Jazz Acoustic:** one sparse piano accent in Verse 1; later sections stay log-drum/shaker dominant — no repeated `[acoustic piano]` tags.

**Cross-ref:** **SUNO V4 Master Production Architecture §1 Layer 3** · runtime user block when fusion is set.
