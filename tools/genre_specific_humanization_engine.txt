## SECTION I: THE GENRE-SPECIFIC HUMANIZATION & AUTHENTICITY ENGINE (LAYER 3)

This module handles linguistic, cultural, and idiomatic thematic profiling across your app's entire ecosystem.

### 1. EDM CATEGORIES (32 SUB-GENRES)
*   **House / Deep House / Soulful House / Tech House / Future House / Disco House:**
    *   *Law:* Short repetitive hooks grounded in **psychological realism** — raw intimacy, relationship tension, physical release. Low-register dry vocals spoken or whispered tight to the capsule.
    *   *Banned:* Narrative country/folk storytelling, sci-fi/rave metaphors (frequency, neon, galaxies, vibrations, dissolving), forced scene backdrops.
    *   *Preferred:* Blunt fragments, internal monologue, conversational honesty — not club-hands-up filler.
*   **Progressive House / Melodic House / Future Bass / Uplifting Trance / Trance:**
    *   *Law:* Emotional release through **felt honesty** — longing, turning points, unrequited love. Floating sung hooks chop-ready in build-ups; sparse breakdown intimacy.
    *   *Banned:* Galaxy/neon poetry (infinite skies, blinding light, starlight, horizons), sci-fi rave clichés, melodramatic festival declarations.
    *   *Preferred:* Short blunt emotional phrases, repetitive singable hooks — psychological state over cinematic travelogue.
*   **Big Room / Big Room Techno / Techno / Hard Techno / Melodic Techno / Acid Techno:**
    *   *Law:* Internal monologues, physical boundaries, sensory overload — hypnotic spoken/whispered delivery against driving kicks.
    *   *Banned:* Sweet pop love stories, sci-fi metaphors (frequency, static tension, neon, seismic), clinical body-horror phrasing.
    *   *Preferred:* Commands, confessions, jagged fragments — vulnerable or hyper-focused psychological state.
*   **Dubstep / Melodic Dubstep / Drum & Bass / Liquid DnB:**
    *   *Law:* Liquid lanes utilize smooth, soulful, half-time R&B vocal flows. Heavy Dubstep and Drum & Bass lanes utilize sharp, high-aggression, rapid MC-style hype commands.
    *   *Banned:* Monotonous jazz-club phrasing or slow, acoustic-guitar singer-songwriter metrics.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Rewind, watch the drop, break the cage, liquid sky, heavy pressure, feel the friction.
*   **Hardstyle / Rawstyle / Hard Bounce / EDM Bounce:**
    *   *Law:* **Dynamic shift mandatory:** [Breakdown] = vulnerable unpolished spoken confession; [Build-up] = cold defiant survival/determination; [Pre-Drop] = ONE yelled/screamed command word before kick impact. Drop = chop/mantra cells only.
    *   *Banned:* Melodramatic clichés (we own the night, ghosts pulling near, strobe light flash), clinical phrasing (hollows out my chest cavity, destroy the grid), sci-fi rave metaphors, cozy mundane filler.
    *   *Preferred:* Honest confession → fierce command arc — e.g. *"The room is spinning"* → *"Look me in the eyes"* → *"GO."*
*   **Nu-Disco / Future Funk / Vinahouse / Amapiano-Vinahouse / Afro House / UK Garage / Jersey Club:**
    *   *Law:* Funk/Disco lanes require retro, high-energy, infectious party-groove hooks. Vinahouse/Jersey variants require hyper-repetitive, rapid, loop-ready vocal micro-chops snapped to punchy kick grids.
    *   *Banned:* Long, sprawling ambient prose or somber, melancholy indie-rock laments.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Hit the floor, bassline bounce, funky rhythm, non-stop, check the clock, step to the beat.

### 2. HIP HOP CATEGORIES (13 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Boom Bap / Hip Hop / Jazz Rap:**
    *   *Law:* Prioritize intricate internal rhyming structures, street-level storytelling, clever double-entendres, and deep metaphorical social analysis.
    *   *Banned:* Monotone electronic whispers or high-energy festival screaming.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Concrete jungle, static needles, raw ink, city blocks, vinyl loops, deep crates.
*   **Trap / Melodic Trap / Drill / UK Drill / NY Drill / Phonk:**
    *   *Law:* Lines must match high-velocity hi-hat rolls or aggressive sliding 808 glides. Drill lines must focus on cold, tactical, high-intensity street narratives. Phonk lines require sinister, low-fidelity tape-loop processing hooks.
    *   *Banned:* Sweet pop-acoustic cliches, soft falsettos, or country-style instrumentation markers.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Cold steel, tracking codes, frozen blocks, zero mercy, baseline phantom, smoke screens.
*   **Lo-Fi Hip Hop / Chillhop / Cloud Rap:**
    *   *Law:* Deliver warm, nostalgic, sleepy, ultra-relaxed internal monologues or hazy, abstract bedroom reflections.
    *   *Banned:* High-register belt screaming, aggressive double-time military drilling cadences.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Midnight rain, coffee steam, faded polaroids, drift away, static skies, slow motion.
*   **Afro-Swing / Afro Rap:**
    *   *Law:* Deliver fluid, melodic rap flows heavily mixed with West African pidgin phrasing and London street vernacular, locked directly to infectious cross-rhythmic pockets.
    *   *Banned:* Stark, abrasive, distorted punk-metal shouting patterns.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Moving sweet, on my wave, standard procedure, lock the lane, daily paper.

### 3. R&B / SOUL CATEGORIES (9 SUB-GENRES)
*   **R&B / Contemporary R&B / 90s R&B / New Jack Swing / Trap Soul:**
    *   *Law:* Write highly expressive, silky, melismatic lines utilizing complex vocal runs. Trap Soul must feature dark, moody, relationship-centered vulnerability. New Jack Swing requires high-tempo, swing-beat dance-pop confidence.
    *   *Banned:* Monotone spoken-word robot text or country bluegrass licks.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Midnight drive, neon shadows, temperature rising, no regrets, silky touch, baseline heartbeat.
*   **Soul / Neo-Soul / Funk / Quiet Storm:**
    *   *Law:* Prioritize deep, organic chest-voice warmth, vintage analog performance dynamics, socially conscious commentary, or smooth, late-night acoustic intimacy.
    *   *Banned:* Hyperpop robotic glitches or harsh metalcore growls.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Natural vibe, velvet skies, inner fire, standard love, electric current, sweet healing.

### 4. POP CATEGORIES (14 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Pop / Mainstream Pop / Pop / Max Martin / Electropop / Dance Pop / Synth Pop:**
    *   *Law:* Enforce absolute precision engineering. Hooks must feature hyper-mathematical line symmetry, immediate melodic earworms, and razor-sharp, pristine commercial tracking patterns.
    *   *Banned:* Intentionally unstructured rambling, unrhymed free-verse, or abrasive industrial distortions.
    *   *Vocabulary:* High voltage, cross the line, target lock, neon lightning, countdown, state of mind.
*   **Bedroom Pop / Indie Pop:**
    *   *Law:* Deliver soft, intimate, unpolished, quirky, raw bedroom vulnerability. Vocals must sound like an un-engineered, close-mic home studio setup.
    *   *Banned:* Massively stacked, polished group harmonies or theatrical arena belting.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Paper-thin walls, taped-up posters, cold tea, dynamic shift, messy room, unspoken thoughts.
*   **K-POP / J-POP / C-Pop / Mandopop:**
    *   *Law:* Construct rapid, multi-lingual code-switch blocks. Alternate frequently between native Asian lyrics and catchy English hook phrases. Structure lyrics to sound like a synchronized multi-member idol group performance.
    *   *Banned:* Monotonous single-vocal solo phrasing without any dynamic arrangement switches.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Starlight eyes, synchronized heart, countdown three-two-one, neon rain, dream chase.
*   **Latin Pop:**
    *   *Law:* Deliver highly romantic, vibrant, rhythmic pop toplines locked directly to infectious acoustic guitar plucking patterns or modern pop-reggaeton grooves.
    *   *Banned:* Dark industrial dystopian content or cold, non-rhythmic indie prose.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Mi amor, bajo el sol, irresistible, dulce veneno, paso a paso, fuego ardiente.
*   **Hyperpop:**
    *   *Law:* Emphasize hyper-saturated, pixelated, chaotic internet-culture imagery, glitchy text processing, and self-referential plastic pop clichés.
    *   *Banned:* Organic acoustic folk warmth or classic traditional blues patterns.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Glitch my heart, plastic crown, laser beams, overload, fully reset, digital candy.

### 5. ROCK / METAL CATEGORIES (16 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Rock / Classic Rock / Hard Rock / Alternative / Alt Rock / Indie Rock:**
    *   *Law:* Target driving, authentic guitar-driven grit, systemic defiance, real room acoustics, and high-energy stadium choruses.
    *   *Banned:* Slick, hyper-quantized electronic club mantras or hip-hop trap triplet code patterns.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Electric static, wire lines, standard deviation, crash down, neon wild, out of gas.
*   **Pop Punk / Emo / Punk:**
    *   *Law:* Deliver fast, punchy, nasal, angst-fueled staccato vocal lines that open up into explosive, melodic, highly energetic anthemic choruses.
    *   *Banned:* Gentle, jazzy melismatic runs or smooth, detached electronic club whispers.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Dead-end town, teenage shadows, broken baseline, status quo, screaming loud, baseline exit.
*   **Metal / Heavy Metal / Metalcore / Death Metal:**
    *   *Law:* Enforce dramatic structural dynamic shifts. Verses must use fast, consonant-heavy staccato patterns over heavy guitars, opening up into soaring clean legato choruses or brutal, guttural breakdown growls.
    *   *Banned:* Bubblegum pop-romance clichés or soft, breathy uncompressed vocal hooks.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Iron gates, shattered glass, systematic crash, cold ash, phantom shadows, razor edges.
*   **Post-Rock / Shoegaze / Dream Pop:**
    *   *Law:* Keep vocals soft, blurry, and deeply integrated into massive walls of ambient guitar distortion, functioning as a warm texture rather than an upfront pop narrative.
    *   *Banned:* Crisp, razor-sharp commercial rap cadences or aggressive crowd-command shouting.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Pale sunlight, blurred windows, shifting waves, echoes, static hiss, faint shadows.

### 6. COUNTRY / FOLK CATEGORIES (8 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Country / Modern Country / Outlaw Country / Americana / Folk-Rock / Bluegrass / Indie Folk / Singer-Songwriter:**
    *   *Law:* Prioritize literal, front-porch narrative storytelling. Establish concrete settings, specific times, real characters, and honest emotional stakes over raw acoustic/organic instrumentation.
    *   *Banned:* Highly processed synthwave imagery, urban street drilling terminologies, or plastic hyperpop cliches.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Dusty highway, screen door, cold rain, dynamic truth, open plains, midnight train.

### 7. GOSPEL / WORSHIP CATEGORIES (12 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Gospel / Traditional Gospel / Contemporary Gospel / Urban Gospel / Southern Gospel / Country Gospel / Afro-Gospel:**
    *   *Law:* Focus on soul-stirring spiritual transformation, massive call-and-response choir stacks, and high-energy vocal improvisations.
    *   *Banned:* Bleak dystopian cynicism, existential despair, or materialist street-bravado themes.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Overflow, heavy chains breaking, joy rising, power in the spirit, dynamic grace, open heavens.
*   **Praise/Worship / Modern Worship / Worship Ballad / CCM / Pop Worship:**
    *   *Law:* Deliver simple, reverent, highly vertical congregational lyrics designed for easy stadium singalongs over soaring, atmospheric instrumental builds.
    *   *Banned:* Rapid-fire technical rap subdivisions or abrasive metalcore growling blocks.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Faithful cornerstone, holy fire, standard surrender, endless wonder, ocean of grace, stand in awe.

### 8. JAZZ / BLUES CATEGORIES (12 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Jazz / Vocal Jazz / Bebop / Smooth Jazz / Jazz Fusion / Fusion / Nu-Jazz / Acid Jazz / Big Band:**
    *   *Law:* Deliver complex, sophisticated micro-timed phrasing that pulls relaxed behind the rhythm grid, incorporating smooth jazz imagery, night-club poetry, or abstract syncopated lines.
    *   *Banned:* Rigidly quantized mechanical metronome counts or aggressive punk-rock delivery.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Velvet midnight, smoking neon, syncopated heartbeat, cool rain, silver smoke, abstract blue.
*   **Blues / Chicago Blues / Delta Blues:**
    *   *Law:* Force the strict traditional 3-line AAB stanza format (Line 1, Line 1 repeated with emotional variance, Line 2 raw thematic payoff). Emphasize deep down-home grit and hard-luck storytelling.
    *   *Banned:* Standard 4-line commercial pop-verse layouts or electronic synthesizer terminologies.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Crossroads, heavy dust, early morning train, worn-out shoes, breaking down, crying out.

### 9. LATIN CATEGORIES (11 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Reggaeton / Dembow / Brazilian Funk / Latin Pop:**
    *   *Law:* Align short, staccato, highly rhythmic vocal phrasing directly with the iconic 16th-note dembow or funk snare loop. Incorporate natural Spanglish code-switching.
    *   *Banned:* Long, sprawling, ambient post-rock essays without clear rhythmic punctuation.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Fuego, dale mami, la discoteca, rompiendo el bajo, sacude el ritmo, bajo las luces.
*   **Bachata / Salsa / Cumbia / Vallenato / Sertanejo / Forró / Bossa Nova:**
    *   *Law:* Bachata requires tragic romance synchronized with syncopated guitar picking. Salsa requires high-tempo narrative verses that erupt into explosive, call-and-response Montuno chorus sections. Bossa Nova requires whispered, conversational night-club jazz poetry.
    *   *Banned:* Aggressive electronic drill slang or loud metalcore vocal screaming patterns.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Corazón herido, amargo destino, repica el tambor, sabrosura, brisa del mar, noche loca.

### 10. REGGAE / DUB CATEGORIES (5 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Reggae / Roots Reggae / Dub / Dancehall / Soca:**
    *   *Law:* Roots/Dub requires conscious spiritual social commentary delivered relaxed behind the beat, leaving wide gaps for tape-delay echo trails. Dancehall/Soca demands explosive carnival energy and rapid dancefloor commands.
    *   *Banned:* Dark industrial techno vocabulary or clinical, sterile cybernetic metaphors.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Positive vibration, sound system, fire burning, rhythm grid, moving massive, elevation.

### 11. AFRO / WORLD CATEGORIES (12 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Afrobeats / Amapiano / Highlife / Fuji / Gqom:**
    *   *Law:* Float fluidly and elastically over deep log-drum frames or polyrhythmic percussion beds, utilizing highly repetitive, infectious pidgin hooks and celebratory group accents.
    *   *Banned:* Gloomy bedroom-pop angst or fast-speed heavy-metal screaming layers.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Standard energy, non-stop vibration, catch the wave, pure magic, heavy baseline.
*   **Bollywood / Filmi / Punjabi / Bhangra / Folk / Middle Eastern / City Pop:**
    *   *Law:* Desi fusions must start with a soaring, long-vowel solo vocal introduction before snapping into rapid, highly rhythmic staccato verses locked to a pounding drum grid. City Pop requires retro-80s neon-lit nightlife sophistication.
    *   *Banned:* Aggressive Western street-drill drillings or harsh industrial noise blocks.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Golden horizon, dhol beating, starry night, neon expressway, dancing shadows.

### 12. CINEMATIC CATEGORIES (13 SUB-GENRES)
*   **Orchestral / Film Score / Cinematic / Trailer / Ambient / Dark Ambient / Ambient Score:**
    *   *Law:* Treat vocals purely as minimalist atmospheric instruments. Keep performance text highly sparse, relying on long, lingering choral chants or deep, spoken trailer narration.
    *   *Banned:* Fast conversational pop chatter or standard verse-chorus top-40 radio pacing.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Eternal void, eclipse, endless time, phantom world, deep shadow, silent expansion.
*   **Synthwave / Retrowave / Vaporwave / New Wave / Industrial / EBM:**
    *   *Law:* Synthwave/Retrowave requires retro-80s dystopian driving moods and neon-tinted highway imagery. Industrial/EBM requires harsh, mechanical, ultra-quantized electronic distortion commands.
    *   *Banned:* Acoustic porch fingerpicking or sunny, cheerful commercial beach pop.
    *   *Vocabulary:* Chrome engines, grid runner, digital horizon, machine protocol, laser drive, static sky.

## NIGERIAN PIDGIN AUTHENTICITY LAYER (when dialect_style_id=nigerian_pidgin)

**OPERATIONAL NOTE:** Never name internal routing infrastructure in output. Pidgin handling is defined entirely by grammar rules below.

### MANDATORY PIDGIN DIALECT LAWS

| English intent | Pidgin rendering | Semantic context |
|---|---|---|
| "I am here (located)" | `na here I dey` | physical presence in space |
| "I am here (emotionally present)" | `I dey here for you` | relational commitment |
| "trouble / chaos" | `wahala` | external mess, conflict |
| "inner turmoil / distress" | `my head no dey ground` | internal mental state |
| "it is ready / finished" | `e don set` | preparedness or completion |
| "don't let..." | `no let...` | prohibition |
| "tired" (object broken down) | `e don tire` | object state |
| "tired" (person exhausted) | `body don weak` | person state |
| "slowly / bit by bit" | `small small` | pacing, gradual change |

**Lyric fourth-wall lock:** Even in Pidgin, **NEVER** name physical instruments or production gear in performable lines.

**Staging vs lyrics:** Pidgin belongs in performable lines and sparse parenthetical ad-libs. Instrumentation and vocal production tags stay in `[staging brackets]` on their own lines.

**Runtime:** User block injects `dialect_style_id=nigerian_pidgin`. Stage 5 compression must preserve Pidgin grammar — never normalize back to standard English.
