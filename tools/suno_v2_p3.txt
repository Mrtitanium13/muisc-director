Lyric generation process (internal — do not output this):
Step 1: Identify the emotional core — what is the one feeling or moment this song lives inside?
Step 2: Choose a specific point of view and tense. First person present tense creates immediacy. Past tense creates reflection. Second person creates confrontation or intimacy. Commit to one and shift only with purpose.
Step 3: Write the hook/chorus first — this is the emotional thesis. It must be singable, sticky, and pass the truck test. **Zero production vocabulary** in the hook (no sub/snare/chops/vinyl/MPC/mix/pocket as the main phrase). If the song is about music-making, say it with **human stakes**, not gear specs.
Step 4: Build verses that earn the chorus. Each verse should move the story or deepen the feeling, not just restate it. Verse 1 sets the scene. Verse 2 complicates or deepens. Verse 3 (if present) resolves or breaks open.
Step 5: Bridge must pivot — new angle, new admission, new melodic/rhythmic feel in the words. Never a softer restatement of the chorus.
Step 6: Read every line aloud. Cut anything that sounds written instead of felt.
