## 2. RHYTHMIC GRID & SYLLABLE QUANTIZATION ROUTING

Do **not** draft line lengths arbitrarily. Every text line must be quantized to the genre's standard rhythmic grid using strict **Syllable-per-Beat (SPB)** economy. **MELODY-SYNC** + genre § (e.g. Hardstyle) enforce caps; this section sets **lane targets**:

- **Low-density / spatial lanes** (Amapiano, House, Club, EDM): Max **1–2 SPB**. Target **2–4 words per line max**. Force wide spaces between lines using empty bars or structural tags. Prioritize staccato, repeating phrases.
- **Medium-density / conversational lanes** (Afrobeats, Pop, Trap, R&B, Country): Max **2–3 SPB**. Target **5–8 words per line max**. Include an organic mid-line break or breathing space for the vocal track.
- **High-density / flow lanes** (Boom Bap, Drill, fast rap): Max **4–5 SPB**. Internal rhyme structures; clip final consonants to stay inside the 16th-note drum grid.

**Auto-correction:** If a line requires more than **4 syllables inside a single 4/4 beat** on a **sung melody** lane, strip secondary adjectives and adverbs immediately.

**Hardstyle / Dubstep:** Fragmented vocal chops · 1–3 word drop anchors — MELODY-SYNC § HARDSTYLE.

---

## 3. SECTION OPTIMIZATION & NARRATIVE ESCALATION

Every section must have a unique narrative and sonic function. Section order → genre roadmaps (`suno_v2_block2_genre_structures.txt`) + **ARRANGEMENT STAGING FORMAT**.

- **Verse 1:** Establish the scene, specific details, and baseline emotion.
- **Pre-Chorus:** Build tension, shift perspective, or accelerate rhythmic pace.
- **Chorus:** The emotional payoff and core hook. Maximum memorability.
- **Verse 2:** **Narrative escalation.** Must introduce new information, a plot twist, or a deeper emotional layer. *Never just restate Verse 1 with swapped words.*
- **Bridge:** The "turn." Shift chord feel, tempo, or lyrical POV. Provide contrast before the final climax.
- **Outro:** Decay, resolution, or DJ mix-out. Avoid neat, thesis-statement endings.

**Anti-static:** Final Chorus must vary — see §4 semantic/staging mutation when commerciality ≥ 7.

---

## 4. VIRALITY, REPLAY VALUE, & SEMANTIC HOOK MUTATION

Activate when internal commerciality is **≥ 7** or user requests viral/radio/hit. Platform psychology → **HIT SONG PSYCHOLOGY** (§2 Audience · §3 Behavioral Hook · §4 Platform Density · `<psychology_audit>` §5).

- **User-Proxy phrasing:** Subconscious confession or relatable boundary — not jokes or ad slogans (Hit Song §3).
- **3-second intro anchor:** Viral / Club / Festival lanes require intro hook in first **3 seconds**.

When commerciality **≥ 7**, the chorus must undergo **semantic mutation** on its final pass. Do **not** mutate by rewriting the core message alone — alter **structural delivery** to simulate a live peak-energy moment:

- **The truncation rule:** Cut the final line of the last chorus short; leave a sudden **1-bar vacuum** of silence before a heavy, unbacked vocal ad-lib or explosive instrumental hit.
- **The call-and-response layering:** Place crowd/chant lines in the **spaces between** main singer lines — `[Chant]` or `[Backing Vocal]` on their own lines, or bare sung parens `(yeah... yeah...)` — **never** `(Choir: ...)`, `(Chant: ...)`, or `(Echo: ...)` labels inside parentheses (Elite §3).
- **The dynamic staging lift:** Elevate the final chorus tag (e.g. mutate `[Chorus: Male Lead]` → `[Final Chorus: Belted Lead, Full Vocal Stack, Anthemic Drop]` per **ARRANGEMENT STAGING FORMAT**).

**Meaning shift:** Final chorus emotional read must still reflect Bridge/Verse 2 revelations (Hit Song §3 semantic mutation). Anti-static: no identical 4-word phrase pasted across the whole chorus.
