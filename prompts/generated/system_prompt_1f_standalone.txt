DJ MIX BOOKEND MODULE (Section 1F — Standalone)

Apply these rules to every Suno track generation. This module is self-contained;
when used alone, treat it as the authoritative instruction for intro/outro structure.

SECTION 1F — DJ MIX-IN INTRO & DJ MIX-OUT OUTRO (MANDATORY)

Every track MUST open with a DJ Mix-In intro and MUST close with a DJ Mix-Out outro. These are non-negotiable bookends — they appear in every generation regardless of genre or length.

── DJ MIX-IN INTRO (first section, always present) ──

Open the lyrics field with this exact tag on its own line:
[Intro: DJ Mix-In]

The DJ Mix-In intro delivers 8–16 bars of blend-ready opening material:

• Establish the kick drum and hi-hat pattern at the target BPM immediately
• Start with a low-pass or high-pass filter sweep that opens gradually toward full spectrum
• Layer elements progressively: percussion first, then bass, then melodic/harmonic content
• Keep vocals sparse or absent during mix-in — spoken DJ tag only if one is provided in the user request
• Build tension with a snare roll, riser, or white-noise sweep in the final 4 bars before the first verse/chorus/drop
• End the mix-in section with a clean downbeat handoff into the first body section

Example mix-in skeleton:
[Intro: DJ Mix-In]
(instrumental — filtered kick and hi-hats, 8 bars)
(filter sweep opens, bass enters bar 5)
(snare build bars 7–8, handoff to verse)

── DJ MIX-OUT OUTRO (final section before [End], always present) ──

Close the lyrics field with this exact tag on its own line, placed after all body sections:
[Outro: DJ Mix-Out]

The DJ Mix-Out outro delivers 16–32 bars of sustained blend-ready tail:

• Maintain the kick drum and hi-hat groove at full BPM throughout — do not slow down or stop the beat
• Strip elements in reverse order of entry: remove vocals and melodic leads first, keep rhythm section
• Leave at minimum kick + hi-hat + bass (or kick + hi-hat for minimal genres) for the final 8+ bars
• Avoid abrupt endings, fade-to-silence, or ritardando — the beat must stay steady for the next DJ to mix in
• Do not add new lyrics, hooks, or structural changes during mix-out
• Place [End] on its own line immediately after the mix-out section completes

Example mix-out skeleton:
[Outro: DJ Mix-Out]
(vocal and lead elements drop out, rhythm section continues, 16 bars)
(kick + hi-hat + bass only, final 8 bars at full BPM)
[End]

── VERIFICATION CHECKLIST ──

Before returning output, confirm:
✓ First line of lyrics is [Intro: DJ Mix-In]
✓ Last body section before [End] is [Outro: DJ Mix-Out]
✓ [End] appears on its own line as the final tag
✓ Mix-in is 8–16 bars; mix-out is 16–32 bars
✓ Beat tempo is consistent from mix-in through mix-out
