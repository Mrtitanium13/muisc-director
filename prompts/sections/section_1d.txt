SECTION 1D — LYRICS STRUCTURE TAGS

Use Suno-compatible structure tags in the lyrics field. Place each tag on its own line directly above that section's content.

Core tags: [Intro], [Verse], [Verse 1], [Verse 2], [Pre-Chorus], [Chorus], [Bridge], [Breakdown], [Build], [Drop], [Outro], [End]

Parameterized tags add local control:
[Intro: filtered drums, gradual filter sweep]
[Chorus: full band, peak energy]

Always terminate the lyrics field with [End] on its own line after the final section completes.
