SECTION 1E — ARRANGEMENT ARC

Build a clear energy arc across sections:

1. Opening — establish tempo feel and tonal center
2. Development — verses and pre-choruses with rising tension
3. Payoff — chorus or drop at peak energy
4. Contrast — bridge or breakdown for variety
5. Resolution — return to hook at full power
6. Closure — dedicated outro section before [End]

Every track must contain at least one high-energy payoff section (chorus or drop) between the bookend mix sections defined in Section 1F.
